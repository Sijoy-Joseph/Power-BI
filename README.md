📊 Financial Report Dashboard 

This Power BI dashboard provides an interactive financial analysis to monitor sales, profit, and performance metrics across different time periods, products, and discount bands.
It helps stakeholders quickly identify trends, segment performance, and business growth opportunities.

⚙️ Data Source

Dataset: financials 

Key Fields:

Sales Data (Gross Sales, Profit, Cost of Goods Sold)

Product Information (Product name, channel partners, discount band)

Date Dimension (Year, Quarter, Month)

Country/Region Data

📈 Dashboard Components
KPI Cards

Gross Sales: 127.93M

Profit: 16.89M

Charts & Visuals

Sum of Sales by COGS (Line Chart):
Tracks sales values over time compared with cost of goods sold.

Profit by Sale Price (Pie Chart):
Shows contribution of different sale prices to overall profit.

Segment Count by Discount Band (Donut Chart):
Breaks down customer segments by applied discount bands (High, Medium, Low, None).

Monthly Sale Price (Bar Chart):
Displays sale price trends across months.

Profit by Quarter (Table):
Quarterly breakdown of sale price values across multiple years.

Country & Product by Discount Band (Clustered Bar Chart):
Compares product sales distribution across discount levels and regions.

Product vs Channel Partners (Table):
Provides sales details by product and channel partner.

🎛 Filters

Country Filter: Allows users to drill into sales and profit for specific countries.

Date Filter: Selectable at daily, monthly, quarterly, or yearly levels for time-based insights.

🛠 How to Use

Open the Power BI report file (.pbix).

Use filters (Country, Date) on the right-hand panel to refine the data view.

Interact with visuals (hover, click, drill down) for deeper insights.

Navigate between Page 1, Page 2, Page 3 for additional reports.

📌 Insights

Sales peaked in certain months (e.g., October, December).

Profit distribution is heavily skewed toward higher sale price ranges.

Medium and low discount bands dominate in terms of count.

Certain products (like Amarilla, Paseo) are top contributors.
